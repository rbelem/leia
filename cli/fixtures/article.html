A Fixture for Reading Aloud

This is a deterministic article used by the leia-ctl CLI to exercise the reader transport end to end. It has a heading, a few paragraphs, and a couple of subheadings so the marching highlight has tokens to walk.

The quick brown fox jumps over the lazy dog. The five boxing wizards jump quickly. Pack my box with five dozen liquor jugs. Sphinx of black quartz, judge my vow.

Second Section

Reading a page aloud means walking its text tokens in order, speaking each, and moving a highlight from phrase to phrase at the pace of the voice. That is the core of Leia.

When a session starts, the background captures the active tab's scope, splits it into tokens, and hands each token to the selected voice engine. The engine streams audio back; the highlight follows.

How to Verify

From the CLI, run leia open-fixture to open this page, then leia start to read it. Observe the leia:session:state and leia:audio:event stream with leia events.
